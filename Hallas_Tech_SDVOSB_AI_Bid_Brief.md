## Claims That Need Confirmation Before Each Submission

Do not make these claims unless supporting documents are attached or the user confirms them:

- SAM.gov active registration status.
- Current manufacturer reseller or partner authorization for a specific OEM.
- Distributor quote validity.
- GSA Schedule, NASA SEWP, CIO-SP, FirstSource, or other vehicle access.
- Past federal contract performance.
- Security clearance, facility clearance, or cleared personnel.
- ISO, CMMI, FedRAMP, SOC 2, or other formal certifications.
- Exact delivery date or license provisioning timeline.
- Any socioeconomic status other than SDVOSB/VOSB/small business.
- Any price reasonableness claim without market, catalog, quote, or prior award support.
